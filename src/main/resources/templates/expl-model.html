Data Explorative Model

Discovering spaces of scarcity and optimizing waste management through data-driven insights.

Key Highlights

  • Problem Addressed: Identifying areas within the city with insufficient waste management resources.
  • Data-Driven Insights: Utilizes geospatial data, population density, and activity heatmaps to pinpoint areas of need.
  • Custom Route Optimization: Generates efficient waste collection paths tailored to high-activity zones.
  • Resource Allocation: Ensures bins and collection resources are deployed where they are needed most.
  • Sustainability Focus: Reduces carbon footprint through optimized routes, minimizing travel distance and time.
